What This Checklist Is For

This is the document that keeps the capstone calm. The capstone is assembled from your labs — and this checklist shows you exactly which lab builds which capstone piece. Every time you finish a lab, you come back here, tick a box or two, and watch the capstone fill in on its own.

💡 WHY A CHECKLIST AND NOT JUST THE BRIEF The Brief tells you what the capstone is. This Checklist tells you where each piece comes from and when. The capstone has six big components and that can feel like a lot to hold in your head. You should not hold it in your head. That is what this list is for — it is the working memory of the project, written down, so your attention goes to building instead of remembering.

Step Zero — Make a Capstone Folder Today

✅ DO THIS — once, right now, on reveal day
  • Make one folder on your computer named CAP2743C_Capstone.
  • Write where it is in the box at the top of this page, so you never lose it.
  • From now on, every time a lab tells you to save a .pbix, also save a copy into this folder.
  • Name the copies clearly — Lab04_themed.pbix, Lab05_interactive.pbix, and so on.
⚠️ THE MISTAKE THIS PREVENTS The students who scramble at the end are not the ones who found the capstone hard. They are the ones whose lab files ended up scattered across a Downloads folder, a Desktop, and a USB stick — and who spent Session 11 hunting for files instead of assembling them. One folder. One place. That single habit is the difference between a calm Session 11 and a stressful one.
