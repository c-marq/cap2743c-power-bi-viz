Visual 4 — Exact Numbers: Matrix

✅ DO THIS
  1. Build a matrix.
  2. Put Category (from Product) in the Rows well.
  3. Put Year (from Date) in the Columns well.
  4. Put Total Sales in the Values well.
  5. Place it on the page.
⚠️ COMMON MISTAKE Building a table instead of a matrix. A table has no Columns well — it can't cross categories against years. Make sure you picked the matrix icon, which gives you both Rows and Columns.

Visual 5 — Parts of a Whole: Pie Chart (Used Correctly)

✅ DO THIS
  1. Build a pie chart — but use it the way Chapter 2 says it CAN work.
  2. Put a field with only a FEW categories in the Legend well — Category from Product (four categories) is a good fit.
  3. Put Total Sales in the Values well.
  4. Place it on the page.
💡 WHY THIS IS THE ONE TIME A PIE IS OK Chapter 2 says a pie works when there are two or three (or a small handful of) categories with clearly different sizes. Four product categories, with Bikes clearly dominant, is right at the edge of where a pie still communicates. Do NOT use Subcategory here — that's ~37 slices, and the chapter showed exactly how that fails.

The Gallery Label Card — Name Each Question · about 30 minutes

A gallery has labels. For each of your six visuals, write the business question it answers — one clear sentence, the kind a manager would actually ask. This is the question→visual skill in reverse: you have the visual, now name the question.
