# Homeworks

Three individual take-home assignments. Each homework consolidates skills from the chapters preceding it. Submitted to Canvas, not back to this repo.

| `Homework1_VisualGallery.html` | Session 4 (Thu May 21) | Sun May 25 by 11:59 PM | Ch 1–2 — multi-page visual gallery with 6+ visual types | 30 |

## What students submit

Each homework asks for two artifacts:

1. **The `.pbix` file** — named `LastName_FirstName_HW#.pbix`
2. **A written response** — 2 short reflection questions embedded in the homework HTML (typed into the textareas, exported to PDF via print)

Both go to Canvas under the homework assignment.

## Submission format pattern

- Homework HTMLs are self-contained — open in browser, fill in answers, print to PDF
- The `.pbix` is built off the most recent lab starter (whichever is most relevant to the homework topic)
- Late policy applies: 48-hour grace, then 10%/day, capped at 30% reduction
- Redo available for any homework scoring below "Good" (revise once for up to Good tier)

## What homeworks are NOT

Homeworks are not net-new content. They consolidate what was taught in the preceding sessions. If a student has done the in-class labs, the homework is "apply the same skills to a new business question, alone, on your own time."
