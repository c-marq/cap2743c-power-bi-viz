## Purpose

This is the script for the **Block A live demo + guided practice** portion of Class 9.
The structure is build-watch-build-replicate: you build each piece slowly while
narrating, students watch, then they replicate it on their own machines while you
circulate.

**Today's demo focus:** Key Influencers, Decomposition Tree, Q&A, and Copilot page generation
